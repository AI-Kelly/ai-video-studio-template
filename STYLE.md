# Style rules

These are the user's rules for how videos look and how on-screen text reads. They start as defaults. During "set up my brand", Claude reads each one to the user in plain English and asks whether to keep it, change it, or drop it, and whether to add any of their own. Claude updates this file with their answers and follows it on every video.

Status: defaults (not yet reviewed with the user)

## Look

- One headline and one visual per scene. Headlines are 3 to 8 words.
- Headlines in sentence case: a plain statement, then one accent-colored italic payoff (written `*like this*` in the cue sheet).
- The accent color is the only highlight: payoff words, highlight boxes, check badges, the one button.
- Background rhythm: dark for the opening and the quote, the second light background every second or third scene, the darkest color for the closing scene.
- Smooth motion only: no bounce, no looping animation.
- No emoji, no exclamation marks, no stock AI imagery.

## On-screen writing

- Plain, everyday words.
- No em dashes.
- No dramatic fragment sequences like "Not X. Not Y. Z."
- Only real numbers and results. Never make one up.
